Privacy Policy

Ozan TikTok Panel

Last updated: 18 November 2026

In short

Ozan TikTok Panel runs on your own computer. Your TikTok data is fetched from TikTok and shown to you; it is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is not sold or used for advertising.

Who we are

Ozan TikTok Panel is a personal tool built and maintained by Harun Yardimci. In this policy “we” means the developer, and “the app” means the Ozan TikTok Panel software.

Information the app accesses

When you connect your TikTok account and grant permission, the app reads the following through TikTok’s official APIs:

  • Basic profile: open ID, display name, username, avatar, bio, verified status and profile link.
  • Account statistics: follower, following, like and video counts.
  • Your public videos: title, description, cover image, link, creation time, duration and view, like, comment and share counts.
  • Posting information: your posting options (privacy levels, interaction settings and maximum video length) so the upload form can show only valid choices.

When you choose to upload a video, the video file and the caption and settings you enter are sent to TikTok to create the post or draft.

How the information is used

Only to show your own account and video statistics to you and to upload videos you choose. The app does not profile you, does not show ads and does not contain analytics or tracking.

Where it is stored

  • Access tokens: the TikTok access and refresh tokens, and your username, display name and avatar link, are stored in a database file on your own computer so you stay signed in. They never leave your computer except in requests to TikTok.
  • Videos you upload: the file is held in a temporary file on your computer only while it is sent to TikTok, then deleted.
  • Statistics and video lists are fetched on demand and are not stored.

We do not operate a server for this app, so we never receive your TikTok data.

Sharing

Your data is exchanged only with TikTok, which is needed for the app to work. TikTok’s own privacy policy applies to data TikTok processes.

The app also has an optional LIVE feature that, when you turn it on, shows gifts received during your own live stream. It connects through the third-party service Euler Stream, which receives your public TikTok username. This feature does not use your TikTok login.

Retention and deletion

  • Click Disconnect in the app to revoke the access token and delete it from your computer.
  • You can also remove access in the TikTok app under Settings › Security › Manage app permissions.
  • Deleting the app’s folder deletes everything it stored.

Children

The app is not directed to children and is meant for creators who meet TikTok’s own age requirements.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page is updated and the date above changes.
