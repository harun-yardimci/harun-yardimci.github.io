Ozan TikTok Panel

TikTok stats & uploads for creators

Your own TikTok stats and uploads, on your own computer.

What it is

Ozan TikTok Panel is a small desktop dashboard that a creator runs on their own computer to look after their own TikTok account. It signs in with TikTok’s official Login Kit and uses TikTok’s official APIs.

📊

Account stats

Follower, following, total like and video counts for the signed-in account.

🎬

Video performance

Views, likes, comments and shares for each of the creator’s public videos, sortable in one grid.

⬆️

Upload videos

Post a video directly, or send it to the TikTok inbox as a draft to finish in the TikTok app. The creator chooses the caption, who can view it and interaction settings every time.

How it works

  • The panel runs locally at localhost on the creator’s own computer. There is no hosted service and no account with us.
  • The creator clicks Connect with TikTok and approves the requested permissions on TikTok’s own page.
  • Disconnecting in the panel revokes the access token. Access can also be removed at any time in the TikTok app under Settings › Security › Manage app permissions.

Permissions used

  • user.info.basic, user.info.profile, user.info.stats: show the profile and account statistics.
  • video.list: list the creator’s public videos with their counts.
  • video.upload, video.publish: upload a video the creator selects, as a draft or as a direct post.
