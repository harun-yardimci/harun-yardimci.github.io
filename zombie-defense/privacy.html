Privacy Policy

Zombie Defense AR

Last updated: 2 November 2026

In short

Zombie Defense AR has no account and no sign-in, and never asks for your name, email or any other personal details. The camera is used only on your device to place the monsters in your room; nothing it sees is recorded, stored or sent anywhere. Your scores and settings stay on your device. The game shows ads through Google AdMob, collects anonymous gameplay statistics with Google Analytics for Firebase, and handles purchases through Apple and RevenueCat.

Camera

The game uses your camera for augmented reality (Apple ARKit): to see your room and place the zombies on your floor. This happens entirely on your device. No images or video are recorded, stored or sent anywhere — not to us and not to anyone else.

Data that stays on your device

The following is kept in the app’s local storage on your device (UserDefaults):

  • Your high scores (your ten best games)
  • The bones you have earned and the upgrades you have bought
  • Your settings: sound, music, haptics, aim sensitivity, left-handed mode and language

None of this is sent to a server. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Anonymous gameplay statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand how the game is played and make it better, the app sends anonymous gameplay statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase.

What is collected:

  • Games played, game length and the wave reached
  • Purchases made
  • App version, device model and coarse country

These statistics are not linked to your identity and are not used to track you.

  • Google Privacy Policy

Third parties

Google AdMob

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob: interstitial ads between games, and optional rewarded ads you can choose to watch for a second wind or double bones.

  • EEA and UK: before ads are shown, Google’s consent form (User Messaging Platform) asks for your choices.
  • App Tracking Transparency: the app shows Apple’s tracking prompt. Only if you allow tracking may AdMob use your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA) to show personalised ads.
  • Changing your mind: “Privacy choices” in the app’s Settings reopens the consent form.
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

In-app purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

The in-app purchases — Full Game and Remove Ads — are paid through Apple. RevenueCat processes the purchase receipts, using an anonymous app user ID, so that your purchases are unlocked in the game.

  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Apple Privacy Policy

Children

The game is rated 9+ and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date above changed.

Türkçe özet

Zombie Defense AR hesap ya da giriş istemez; adınızı, e-postanızı veya başka bir kişisel bilginizi sormaz.

  • Kamera: Yalnızca cihazınızda, artırılmış gerçeklik (ARKit) için kullanılır. Görüntü ya da video kaydedilmez, saklanmaz, hiçbir yere gönderilmez.
  • Cihazda kalan veriler: En iyi on oyununuzun skorları, kazandığınız kemikler ve aldığınız geliştirmeler, ayarlarınız (ses, müzik, titreşim, nişan hassasiyeti, solak modu, dil). Uygulamayı silmek bunların tamamını siler.
  • Reklam (Google AdMob): Oyunlar arasında geçiş reklamları ve isteğe bağlı ödüllü reklamlar (ikinci şans, çift kemik). AEA ve Birleşik Krallık’ta Google’ın onay formu, ayrıca Apple’ın izleme izni sorulur; yalnızca izin verirseniz AdMob kişiselleştirilmiş reklam için reklam kimliğinizi (IDFA) kullanabilir. Ayarlar’daki “Privacy choices” onay formunu yeniden açar.
  • İstatistik (Google Analytics for Firebase): Oynanan oyun, oyun süresi, ulaşılan dalga, yapılan satın almalar, uygulama sürümü, cihaz modeli ve kaba ülke bilgisi. Kimliğinize bağlanmaz, izleme için kullanılmaz.
  • Satın almalar: Full Game ve Remove Ads, Apple üzerinden ödenir; RevenueCat, satın almaları açmak için makbuzları anonim bir kullanıcı kimliğiyle işler.
  • Çocuklar: Oyun 9+ yaş derecelidir ve 13 yaş altı çocuklara yönelik değildir.
